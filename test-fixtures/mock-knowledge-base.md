# Northwind Robotics — Field Service Handbook

## Company

Northwind Robotics was founded in 2011 by Dr. Priya Raghunathan and Marcus Feld in Tallinn, Estonia.
The company builds autonomous warehouse inspection drones. Its flagship product line is the
Kestrel series. Headquarters moved to Rotterdam in 2019 after the Series B round of 42 million euro
led by Meridian Ventures.

## Products

The Kestrel K2 is the entry-level inspection drone. It carries a 12 megapixel visual sensor,
flies for 22 minutes on a single charge, and weighs 840 grams. Its list price is 7,400 euro.

The Kestrel K4 Pro adds a thermal imaging payload and LiDAR. It flies for 31 minutes,
weighs 1,260 grams, and lists at 18,900 euro. The K4 Pro requires firmware 4.2 or later.

The Kestrel K7 Enterprise is the fleet model. It supports hot-swap batteries, giving effectively
continuous operation, and lists at 46,000 euro per unit with a mandatory 12,000 euro annual
support contract.

## Warranty policy

Every Kestrel ships with a 24 month limited warranty covering manufacturing defects.
Battery cells are covered for 9 months or 400 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
Crash damage is excluded unless the customer purchased the Aegis Cover add-on, which costs
15 percent of the unit list price per year and covers two crash repairs annually.

## Service levels

Standard support responds within 2 business days. Priority support responds within 4 hours
and is bundled with every K7 Enterprise contract. Onsite dispatch is available in the Benelux
region only, with a 6 hour target arrival window.

## Firmware

Firmware 4.0 shipped in March 2022 and introduced obstacle mesh mapping.
Firmware 4.2 shipped in November 2022 and added thermal payload support required by the K4 Pro.
Firmware 5.0 shipped in June 2024, dropped support for the discontinued Kestrel K1,
and reduced average battery drain by 11 percent across the K2 and K4 Pro.

## Compliance

All Kestrel units are certified under EASA Open Category A2. Operators must hold an A2
Certificate of Competency. Flights above 120 metres require a Specific Category authorisation,
which Northwind does not provide. Data captured by the thermal payload is classed as
personal data under GDPR when it can identify an individual, and must be retained no longer
than 30 days by default.
